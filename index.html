The Carta de Lei of 1867 in Akoma Ntoso

Portugal’s Carta de Lei of July 1, 1867, which approved the penal and prison reform of that year. Article 1 of the reform reads Fica abolida a pena de morte: the death penalty is abolished. The title it sits under concerns civil crimes. The law is marked up in Akoma Ntoso, the OASIS standard for legal and legislative documents, and styled after the printed collection of laws. The text is in Portuguese.

How it was made

The XML was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini from a scan of the printed collection of the laws of 1867, proofread against the page images. Claude didn’t write a word of this text, and the printer’s numbering errors are left as they are. The README lists them.
