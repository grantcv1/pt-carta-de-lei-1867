# The Carta de Lei of 1867 in Akoma Ntoso

Portugal's Carta de Lei of July 1, 1867, in Akoma Ntoso XML, with a CSS stylesheet that renders it after the printed collection of laws. The law approves the penal and prison reform attached to it, whose Article 1 abolishes the death penalty. It was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini.

The text is in Portuguese, with no translation.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `carta-de-lei-1867.xml` | The Akoma Ntoso document |
| `carta-de-lei-1867.css` | The stylesheet, linked from the XML |
| `index.html` | Landing page for GitHub Pages |

To view the document locally, keep the XML and CSS files in the same folder and open the XML in a browser.

## About the markup

- **Standard:** Akoma Ntoso 2.0 (AKN 3.1). The namespace is `http://docs.oasis-open.org/legaldocml/ns/akn/3.0`, with `version="3.1"` on the root element.
- **Validation:** The XML validates against the Akoma Ntoso 2.0 cs01 schema.
- **Structure:** The two-article law is an `<act name="cartaDeLei">`. The reform that "faz parte" of it is an `<attachment>` holding a `<doc name="reforma">`, with `<title>`, `<article>`, `<paragraph>`, `<list>` and `<point>` elements.
- **`intro` and `content`:** Text followed by a list or paragraph is in `<intro>`. Other text is in `<content>`.
- **Cross-references:** References between articles of the reform are `<ref>` elements that link to the article cited, 41 in all. References to the codigo penal are not linked.
- **Highlight:** Article 1 has `class="abolition"`, and the stylesheet sets it large. The emphasis is editorial and is not in the source.
- **Identifiers:** Every element with an `eId` also has a `GUID`. Text-level `<p>` elements have neither.

## Left as printed

The text follows the print, including its spelling, accents and punctuation. Words broken across lines by a hyphen are rejoined. The following look like errors in the print and were not corrected:

| Where | As printed | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Third title | `TITULO II` | Repeats Titulo II. Its `eId` ends in `tit_II_2` to stay unique |
| After Title II | No Titulo IV or V | The numbering goes from II to VI |
| After Title XI | No Titulo XII | The numbering goes from XI to XIII |
| Art. 53 | No number | The text runs from Art. 52.° to Art. 54.°, and the unnumbered article has no `<num>`. Its `eId` ends in `art_53` |
| Title I heading | `d'estas pena` | Suspected: "penas" |
| Art. 21 | `instrucçãomora e lisação` | Suspected: "instrucção e moralisação" |
| Art. 40 | `[construidas` | A stray bracket, or possibly a mark on the page |
| Art. 50, n.° 5 | `medicoeleito` | Suspected: "medico eleito" |
| Art. 54 | `artigos 43.°, 51.° e n.° 1.°` | Left as printed |

These readings were made by eye from a 300 dpi scan. The page was first read by OCR, then corrected against the images. Some small marks may be specks rather than punctuation, for example in Art. 59 § 2, where "e para elle" is a reading of a faint letter.

## Source

The text comes from a [scan of a printed collection of laws](https://comemoracoes150anosabolicaopenamorte.fd.uc.pt/pdf/lei.pdf), pages 269 to 274, on the University of Coimbra Faculty of Law's site for the 150th anniversary of the abolition. The page cites the D. de L. (taken here to be the Diário de Lisboa) n.° 153, of July 13. The scan does not name the collection. The signed original is in the Torre do Tombo, and it was not used for this edition.
